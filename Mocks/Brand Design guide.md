## Brand & Style
The design system establishes an editorial, high-trust appointment concierge aesthetic tailored for discerning urban consumers in Pune. It blends modern architectural geometry with timeless editorial restraint, evoking the poise of bespoke heritage hospitality and private clinical practices.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Audience:** Discerning professionals, wellness seekers, and high-standard service consumers who value discretion, punctual execution, and vetted craft over gamified marketplaces.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Quiet confidence, calm efficiency, and complete structural transparency. Zero dark patterns, zero artificial urgency banners, and zero superficial community fluff.

### Visual Movement
- **Editorial Modernism:** Warm ivory foundations paired with deep cobalt ink typography, razor-sharp architectural borders, and generous breathing room. High editorial precision replaces standard transactional SaaS templates.

## Colors
The color architecture relies on a luminous, warm ivory environment anchored by authoritative deep cobalt, delicate teal status cues, and deep slate ink for typographic legibility.

### Surface Tones
- **Base Canvas:** `#FAF9F5` (Warm Ivory Canvas) creates a welcoming, organic baseline instead of harsh sterile whites.
- **Surface Layer 1:** `#F5F3ED` (Subtle Recessed Surface) for input wells, disabled states, and subtle grouping containers.
- **Surface Tinted:** `#F0F5FA` and `#E2ECF6` (Soft Cobalt Tint) for highlighted appointment slots, active dates, and selected state cards.

### Core Brand & Ink
- **Primary Anchor:** `#1A365D` (Deep Cobalt) serves as the primary touchpoint for structural navigation, primary CTA backgrounds, and executive-level badges.
- **Interactive Blue:** `#1E429F` for interactive states, focused links, and active stepper nodes.
- **Deep Ink Text:** `#0F172A` delivers uncompromising optical clarity across all text, numerals, and currency symbols (`₹`).
- **Muted Text:** `#64748B` provides balanced contrast for auxiliary descriptions and metadata.

### Accents & Borders
- **Teal Accent:** `#0D9488` (Primary Teal) and `#14B8A6` (Vibrant Teal) reserved strictly for verified status indicators, confirmed booking receipts, and critical progress cues.
- **Structural Outlines:** `#E2E8F0` provides crisp, hairline definition across all cards and dividers.

## Typography
Typographic rhythm balances the editorial gravitas of Newsreader with the pristine geometric utility of Plus Jakarta Sans.

### Hierarchy & Usage
- **Newsreader (Display & Headlines):** Used for venue titles, practitioner names, category hero banners, and booking confirmation titles. Evokes the trusted permanence of high-end journals and historic Pune institutions.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (UI, Labels & Numeric Data):** Deployed for all operational text, interactive touchpoints, time stamps, duration values, and Indian Rupee prices (`₹`). Numbers render with tabular-lining characteristics to ensure precision when comparing schedules and rates.
- **Text Alignment:** Always left-aligned or contextual. Never center-align service descriptions or booking parameters.

## Layout & Spacing
A fluid 4-column layout on mobile scales to 8 columns on tablet and 12 columns on desktop web contexts, rooted in an 8pt spatial baseline.

### Breakpoints & Fluidity
- **Mobile (`< 640px`):** Single-column flow with a strict `1rem` (16px) margin. Touch target bounding boxes maintain a minimum height and width of 48px to preserve usability while traveling or in transit.
- **Tablet (`640px – 1024px`):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and gutters. Master-detail screens reveal practitioner details alongside booking availability sidebars.
- **Desktop (`> 1024px`):** Max container width bounded at `1180px` to maintain focused readability, flanked by `3rem` margins.

### Vertical Rhythm
- Standard element gaps rely on `space-md` (16px) within card groups.
- Category modules and date picker shelves use `space-xl` (32px) top and bottom separation to eliminate clutter.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is articulated primarily through layered tonal surfaces and crisp `#E2E8F0` hairline borders rather than heavy blur cascades.

### Layer Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** `#FAF9F5` flat surface.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Surface):** `#FFFFFF` framed by a `1px` solid `#E2E8F0` border and an ambient micro-shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Appointment / Hover State):** Tinted `#F0F5FA` or `#FFFFFF` elevated with `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(26, 54, 93, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(26, 54, 93, 0.04)` and `#CBD5E1` border stroke.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Bottom Booking Sheets):** `#FFFFFF` with an elevated scrim: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`. Background scrim uses `#0F172A` at 40% opacity with a subtle `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`.

## Shapes
A unified curved geometry imparts an approachable luxury touch across tactile UI elements while maintaining architectural structure.

### Radius Distribution
- **Cards, Sheets & Overlays:** `1rem` (16px, `rounded-2xl`) on core service cards, booking modules, and modal containers.
- **Buttons, Inputs & Interactive Slots:** `0.75rem` (12px, `rounded-xl`) to form cohesive, comfortable touch targets.
- **Chips, Category Tags & Status Pills:** Fully rounded `9999px` (pill format) for high contrast against structured rectangular cards.
- **Dividers & Strokes:** Razor-thin `1px` outlines across all card borders to maintain crisp edge resolution on high-density retina displays.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Confirm / Reserve):** Solid `#1A365D` background, text in crisp `#FFFFFF`, `rounded-xl`, height `48px`, horizontal padding `24px`. On tap/hover: transitions to `#1E429F`.
- **Secondary Action (Change Slot / Details):** Transparent background with `1px` solid `#E2E8F0` border, `#1A365D` text, transitioning to `#F0F5FA` background on touch.
- **Destructive Action:** Text in `#DC2626` on `#FEF2F2` background, with no heavy shadow.

### Service & Practitioner Cards
- Containers use `#FFFFFF` fill with `1px` solid `#E2E8F0` border and `rounded-2xl` corners.
- Internal hierarchy: Newsreader font for the provider/business title (`headline-sm`), geometric sans for Pune location tags (e.g., "Koregaon Park", "Kalyani Nagar"), professional tenure markers, and unambiguous pricing (`price-md`).
- Strict rule: Absence of star rating rows, review tally badges, or discount tags. Replaced with clear practitioner credentials and specific service duration tags (e.g., "60 mins • ₹2,400").

### Date & Time Slot Selectors
- **Inactive / Available Slot:** `#FFFFFF` background, `1px` solid `#E2E8F0` border, `rounded-xl`, centered `label-md` in `#0F172A`. Minimum height `44px`.
- **Selected Slot:** Solid `#1A365D` fill with white text, or soft `#F0F5FA` container with a `1.5px` border in `#1E429F` and `#1A365D` text.
- **Unavailable Slot:** `#F5F3ED` background, text `#94A3B8`, border `#E2E8F0`, pointer-events disabled.

### Filter & Category Chips
- Unselected: Surface `#F5F3ED`, `#0F172A` text, height `36px`, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `12px 16px`.
- Selected: `#1A365D` fill with `#FAF9F5` text.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Surface: `#FFFFFF` bounded by `1px` `#E2E8F0` border. Height `48px`, internal padding `12px 16px`, `rounded-xl`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#1A365D` with an accessible `0 0 0 3px rgba(26, 54, 93, 0.12)` glow.
- Labels: Placed above fields in `label-md` with `#0F172A`.

### Appointment Summary Receipt & Sticky Bottom Bar
- Sticky mobile checkout panel docked at bottom of the viewport using `#FFFFFF` with a top border in `#E2E8F0`, elevated by Level 2 shadow.
- Displays total appointment fee in `price-lg` (`₹`) alongside a full-width "Confirm Booking" primary button.
